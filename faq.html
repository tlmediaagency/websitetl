FAQ

Straight answers

What partnership looks like — without the VA brochure language.

What is a Strategic Operations Partner?

It is not a task-taker. TL Media Agency integrates the backend of your business — systems, team logistics, communications, and operating cadence — so the founder is no longer the bottleneck. Written, we are your Strategic Operations Partner. In conversation: your Strategic Operations Integrator.

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?

A typical VA waits for a list. We own outcomes. That means systems design, team leadership, HR workflows, financial admin, and follow-through — plus creative when the brand has to match the operation. You are hiring a partner, not a ticket queue.

Who is this built for?

Established entrepreneurs, small business owners, and CEOs who need an executive-level operator for systems, team logistics, and day-to-day backend chaos.

What are the retainers?

Three: Focused Ops Sprint, Strategic Integrator Retainer, and Fractional Operations Manager. Creative — design, web, and Remember Me Photography by T — sits under the same roof.

Who will I work with?

Tamaya Livingston leads as Founder and Strategic Operations Partner. Tayjah is Brand Operations Associate for clothing labels and mission-driven founders. Kevin leads digital presence.

What is Brainstorm Brew?

A focused working session with Tamaya. You leave with a usable next move — not a pitch deck.

Do I need a long-term contract?

Sprints are scoped. Retainers are ongoing because operations is not a one-week project. We outline the commitment in plain language before anything begins.

How do we start?

Book a strategy call, or start with Brainstorm Brew. You will leave with a recommendation — sprint, retainer, or fractional.

Where are you based?

Fully remote. Tamaya leads the firm; Tayjah works from the Houston area.

How is pricing structured?

By altitude: sprint, retainer, or fractional. After we understand the operating load, you get a clear recommendation.

Still have a question? Ask on a strategy call.
