FAQ

Common questions

Straightforward answers so you know what to expect.

What exactly does TL Media Agency do?

We provide virtual assistant support (calendar, email, operations) and graphic design / brand services (identity, marketing materials, social content support, startup foundations). The goal is simple: free you from the tasks that drain time and energy so you can focus on growth and the work only you can do.

Who is this best for?

Small and medium business owners, people turning hobbies or passion projects into businesses, entrepreneurs who find the tech/admin side overwhelming, and established businesses that need reliable creative + operational support without building a large internal team yet.

What is Brainstorm Brew?

Brainstorm Brew is a quick session for picking Tamaya’s brain about your business ideas. Bring the messy thoughts, the almost-ready offer, or the “I don’t know where to start” — walk away with clearer next steps.

Do I need a long-term contract?

We start with a conversation and a clear recommendation. Many clients continue on an ongoing basis because the support becomes part of how they operate, but we keep the process flexible. We’ll outline options clearly so you know what you’re committing to.

How do we get started?

Use the contact form to inquire, or book a Brainstorm Brew if you want a focused idea session first. We’ll follow up personally.

What if I’m not tech-savvy?

That’s one of the people we love working with most. Many clients are excellent at their craft or service and simply don’t want (or need) to master every platform. We handle the tech-heavy and admin-heavy pieces and communicate clearly — no jargon dumps.

Do you only do design, or only admin?

Both. Some clients need pure administrative support. Others need brand and marketing assets. Many need a mix. We’ll focus on the highest-leverage combination for your situation rather than forcing a package that doesn’t fit.

Where are you based / is support remote?

Support is fully remote. We work with clients wherever they are and communicate via the tools that already fit your workflow (email, messaging, shared calendars, etc.).

Do you offer resources beyond done-for-you support?

Yes. There are ebooks on choosing and maximizing a team, managing social media, and using AI for efficiency and growth at tlmedia.gumroad.com.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing depends on scope (hours, mix of admin vs. creative, one-time projects vs. ongoing). After we understand your needs on the discovery call, you’ll receive a clear recommendation. Transparency is part of elevated support.

Still have a question? Just ask.
