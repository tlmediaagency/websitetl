About

The Partner Behind the Business Owner

TL Media Agency does more than check off tasks. We become your partner.

Founder · Strategic Operations Partner

Tamaya Livingston

I’m the founder of TL Media Agency — a strategic operations firm for entrepreneurs who need the backend of the business to run as well as the vision.

Since 2022 we have existed for one reason: take the operational load off the founder. Not by collecting tasks. By integrating systems, leading team logistics, and installing a cadence you can actually grow on.

The firm started as a leap of faith after I left the mortgage industry and chose the operations and design work I had already been doing on the side. That same seriousness — prayer, partnership, follow-through — is how we still show up.

What I love most is watching a founder step out of the backend and back into the work only they can do. I don’t do it alone. Tayjah and Kevin are operators on this team, matched to the work.

Our story

The TLM Brand Story

Every entrepreneur dreams of scaling their vision, of seeing their passion take flight. Yet, too often, that dream gets bogged down by the daily grind, the overwhelming tech, and the endless “to-do” lists. You, the visionary, find yourself juggling everything, feeling the pressure mount, and seeing your brilliant ideas gather dust. You’re an idea-generator, a leader, a passionate business owner – but what you truly need is a trusted partner to bridge the gap between inspiration and impactful execution.

That’s where TLM steps in.

Our journey began not with a grand master plan, but with a simple act of genuine help – transforming a friend’s spreadsheet in minutes, sparking the realization that true support is invaluable. It ignited a passion for helping others achieve their “aha!” moments, turning potential into tangible progress. This humble beginning, rooted in joy and a natural talent for organization and creativity, quickly evolved. We realized that while we’d laugh and celebrate small wins, our clients needed more than just enthusiasm; they needed elevated, professional partnership that was not to be taken lightly.

At the heart of TLM is the Lioness – a symbol of strategic focus, unwavering support, and natural leadership. Like the lioness, we don’t just act; we hunt for the best results for your business, serving as your dedicated partner who helps lead your vision to fruition. This pursuit is rooted in our belief that with faith, all things are possible, and that true growth comes from opportunity and divine guidance. Our philosophy is simple yet profound: if everyone connected to us is a millionaire, then we are, too. We empower you to elevate your mindset for your business, just as ours does when we work together. When God is for us, growth truly feels effortless.

We understand the misconception that virtual assistants “know everything” or are meant to be the “boss.” At TLM, we respectfully challenge that. We are your diligent researchers, your meticulous organizers, and your thought-sorters, providing insightful input while ensuring you remain in confident control. We free up your most valuable asset – time – by untangling the organizational knots that create the illusion of overwhelm. Your journey with us is one of transformation: from handling everything yourself to having a strategic partner who empowers your vision without ever taking the reins.

Our impact extends beyond tasks. We aim to be the go-to referral for small and medium businesses seeking high-quality, accessible growth options that don’t break the bank. We strive to help you find the delicate balance between ambition and passion, fostering success that feels both sustainable and joyful. Through our partnership, you’ll feel confident, reassured, and truly cared for, receiving a service that is priceless.

At TLM, we are building a legacy – a structure that will enable us to reach clients across time zones, empower future generations with entrepreneurship, and continue to champion the belief that everyone can benefit from help to see the greater potential in what they already have.
