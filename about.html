About

The heart behind the work

A small team of virtual assistants — support that feels like a real partnership.

Founder

Tamaya Livingston

I’m the heart behind TL Media Agency — a virtual assistant and graphic design firm dedicated to empowering small and medium-sized businesses (and passionate creators turning hobbies into real businesses).

Since 2022 we’ve focused on one mission: free entrepreneurs from the often-overwhelming day-to-day tasks so they can reconnect with their core passions, focus on strategic growth, and actually have a personal life.

TL Media Agency was born out of a personal leap of faith. After being let go from a role in the mortgage industry, I chose to fully embrace the virtual assistant and design work I had already been doing on the side. That decision — guided by prayer and the support of people who believed in me — is the spirit we bring to every client.

What I love most is seeing the tangible impact: a hobby becoming a thriving business, or a founder finally able to step back from the tech and admin so they can do the work only they can do. I don’t do it alone — Tayjah and Kevin are part of the elevated support we deliver.
