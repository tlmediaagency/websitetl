Typography

Display

Cormorant Garamond

Weights 500, 600. Headings and wordmark. Tracking +0.02em (hero titles +0.08em). Fallback: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif.

Body

Outfit

Weights 300 (taglines), 400 (body), 500 (nav/buttons), 600 (emphasis). Line-height 1.6. Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif.

Google Fonts: Cormorant+Garamond:wght@500;600 and Outfit:wght@300;400;500;600

Radius & motion

Radius: 8 / 12 / 16 / 24px. Buttons are pills (9999px), height 36 / 44 / 48px. Motion 200ms ease-out. Press scale 0.98. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.
