Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

TL Media Agency ("we," "us," "our") operates tlmediallc.net (the "Site"). This page explains what information we collect from visitors and clients, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

TL Media Agency is headquartered in Slidell, Louisiana, and operates as a fully remote strategic operations and creative firm. You can reach us at contact@tlmediallc.net.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly. When you fill out a form on this Site — such as a Strategy Call or Brainstorm Brew request — we collect what you provide, which may include your name, email address, business details, and anything you write in the message field. These forms are processed through HoneyBook, our client management platform.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site — pages viewed, time on site, general location (city/region level, not precise address), device type, and how you arrived at the Site (for example, from a search engine or social media). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this.

How we use this information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule calls or sessions
  • To provide the services you've requested or contracted for
  • To understand how people find and use the Site, so we can improve it
  • To send you information you've directly requested (we do not add you to marketing emails without your consent)

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Third parties we work with

We use a small number of outside services to run this Site and our client work:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic and usage analytics. Google's Privacy Policy
  • HoneyBook — form submissions, scheduling, and client management. HoneyBook's Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website's files. GitHub's Privacy Statement

Each of these services has its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies

This Site uses cookies placed by Google Analytics to distinguish visitors and understand usage patterns. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so may not affect your ability to use the Site, though some analytics may be less accurate.

Data retention

Information submitted through our forms is retained as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or fulfill a client engagement, or as required by law. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, request that we correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@tlmediallc.net. Depending on where you're located, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA (California) — reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.

Children's privacy

This Site and our services are directed at businesses and adult professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your information? Reach us at contact@tlmediallc.net.
