FAQ

Straight Answers

What partnership looks like — without the VA brochure language.

What does TL Media Agency do, in plain English?

We partner with founders, owners and CEOs of small companies on the technical side of their business: email, social media, scheduling, paperwork, systems, team tasks and more. That way you can get back to what you love without the heavy overhead and responsibility. Unlike a typical virtual assistant, we learn how your business runs and become your partner. You can start with a $7, 15-minute Brainstorm Brew, book a 45-minute Strategy Call, hire us for a one-off project, or work with us on an ongoing basis.

What is a Strategic Operations Partner?

It is not a task-taker. TL Media Agency integrates the backend of your business (systems, team logistics, communications and operating cadence) so the founder is no longer the bottleneck. In writing we are your Strategic Operations Partner. In conversation: your Strategic Operations Integrator.

What does TL Media Agency do?

We run the operating side of established businesses: systems and workflow design, executive calendar and communications, team logistics and HR workflows, financial admin, vendor and client logistics, and digital presence. Design, web and photography sit under the same roof.

How does a Strategic Operations Partner differ from a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant takes tasks off your plate, and we do that too. A Strategic Operations Partner goes a step further. We own the outcome, not just the task: we design the systems behind the work, lead team and vendor logistics, handle HR workflows and financial admin, follow through until it is done, and bring creative in when the brand has to match the operation. You stay in control as the decision-maker, with a partner who researches, organizes, sorts through the options and tells you what we recommend. Think of it as extra hands plus the plan, the process and the accountability behind them.

Why would I still need an operator or virtual assistant if I use AI?

AI is good at drafting, summarizing and automating individual tasks, and we use it where it helps. What it does not do is own the outcome: decide priorities, manage people and vendors, keep commitments, fix the process when it breaks, and be accountable to you week after week. Someone also has to design the systems AI plugs into and keep them running. That owner is the operator. Many founders find AI makes a good operator more effective, not unnecessary.

What is a fractional operations manager?

A fractional operations manager is senior operating capacity without a full-time executive hire. At TL Media Agency that seat includes a weekly founder briefing, team leadership and accountability cadences, systems optimization, and KPI visibility.

Can TL Media Agency act as an integrator or fractional COO?

Yes. Our Strategic Integrator Retainer places an operator inside your business, and the Fractional Operations Manager seat covers senior operations leadership. Which one fits depends on the operating load, which we diagnose on a strategy call.

Who is this built for?

Established entrepreneurs, small business owners and CEOs who need an executive-level operator for systems, team logistics and day-to-day backend work. We also serve clothing brands and mission-driven founders, and teams that outgrew DIY operations.

What are the retainers?

Three altitudes: Focused Ops Sprint (a defined problem, mapped, installed and handed off), Strategic Integrator Retainer (an ongoing operator inside the business), and Fractional Operations Manager (senior operating capacity without a full-time hire).

What is a KPI, and how do you help set them?

A KPI (key performance indicator) is a number that shows whether part of your business is on track toward a specific goal. Examples include monthly revenue, how quickly leads get a response, on-time project delivery, or repeat-client rate. We help you decide which few KPIs matter for your stage and goals, set a baseline and a target for each, and build a simple way to track them, so decisions rest on measurements instead of guesses.

What happens in a Focused Ops Sprint?

We audit where time, money and follow-through leak, build the SOPs and workflows across the tools you already pay for, design your inbox, calendar and communications command, and finish with a 30-day cadence, dashboards and a clean handoff your team can keep.

What is Brainstorm Brew?

A focused working session with Tamaya, not a sales pitch. We name whether the bottleneck is systems, people or priorities, you leave with a simple operating plan, and you learn whether a sprint, retainer or fractional seat is the right next step.
